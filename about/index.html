About Ink of Sinai

The Torah, up close

Ink of Sinai makes videos and articles about the Hebrew Bible in its original language: what its words mean, how its stories are built, and the details most readers never notice. The videos are on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The Thursday email covers each week's Torah portion.

How we check

One text. Every Hebrew word, verse and count on this site comes from the Westminster Leningrad Codex, the standard digital edition of the Leningrad Codex, the oldest complete manuscript of the Hebrew Bible.

Counted, not remembered. When a page says a word appears a number of times, or “only” in certain verses, that number comes from a computer search of every verse.

The Bible as the source. We explain the Hebrew Bible from its own text. If we can't show it in the verse, we don't say it.

God's name. We don't print God's four-letter name in Hebrew. Verses that contain it are quoted in English.

Corrections. If you find a mistake, reply to any Thursday email or comment on the video, and we'll fix the page.
