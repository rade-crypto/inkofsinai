About Ink of Sinai

The Torah, up close

Ink of Sinai is an independent channel about the Hebrew Bible in its original language: what its words mean, how its stories are built, and the patterns that run through it. The videos are on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and a free email goes out every Thursday.

We aren't part of any organization. Everything here comes from the Hebrew text itself.

Found a mistake? Reply to any Thursday email or comment on a video, and we'll fix it.
