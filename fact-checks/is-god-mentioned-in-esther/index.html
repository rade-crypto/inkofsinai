Is God mentioned in the book of Esther?

What the Hebrew text says, and the four places people point to

By Ink of SinaiChecked against the Hebrew textHow we checkPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

No. None of God's names appears in any of the 167 verses of Esther. But four times, four words in a row begin or end with the letters of God's four-letter name, the name the book never writes out (Esther 1:20, 5:4, 5:13 and 7:7).

The four places

In each, take one letter from each of four words in a row: the first letters or the last letters, read forwards or backwards. Each time, they spell God's four-letter name. We don't print that name in Hebrew on this site, so the words are shown without marking the letters.

Esther 5:4יָבוֹא הַמֶּלֶךְ וְהָמָן הַיּוֹםfirst letters, read forwards“let the king and Haman come today”

Esther 7:7כִּי־כָלְתָה אֵלָיו הָרָעָהlast letters, read forwards“that evil was determined against him”

Could it be chance?

We checked the rest of the Hebrew Bible for the same thing: four words in a row whose first or last letters spell the name, forwards or backwards. It happens about once every 2,500 words. Esther has 3,045 words, so you would expect about one. It has four. By chance alone, four or more would happen about 3 or 4 times in 100.

“From another place”

When Mordecai presses Esther to go to the king, he says that if she stays silent, relief and rescue will come to the Jews מִמָּקוֹם אַחֵר, “from another place” (Esther 4:14). The book never says where that place is.

Esther is read at Purim, which begins the evening of March 22, 2027.

Questions people ask

Is God mentioned in Esther?

No. No name or title of God appears in any of its 167 verses.

Where is God's name hidden in Esther?

In Esther 1:20, 5:4, 5:13 and 7:7, where the first or last letters of four words in a row spell God's four-letter name.

When is the book of Esther read?

At Purim. In 2027, Purim begins the evening of March 22.

Why doesn't Esther mention God?

The text doesn't say. Mordecai's “from another place” (Esther 4:14) is as close as it comes.
