Does the Bible say “fear not” 365 times?

We counted every “do not fear” in the Hebrew Bible

By Ink of SinaiPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

No. In the Hebrew Bible, אַל־תִּירָא (“do not fear”) and its plural and feminine forms appear 75 times, in 72 verses. Adding “you shall not fear” and every other “do not be afraid” command brings the total to 123, about a third of 365.

How we counted: every verse of the Hebrew Bible, for each phrase in all its forms (to one man, to a woman, to a group). Some verses hold two of these commands, so we count times, not verses.

The first “fear not”

The first time the Bible says it, God says it to Abram in a vision: אַל־תִּירָא אַבְרָם, “do not fear, Abram” (Genesis 15:1). The first time a woman hears it is Hagar in the desert, crying over her dying son: אַל־תִּירְאִי (Genesis 21:17).

So where does 365 come from?

Not from the Hebrew text. No form of the phrase, counted any way we tried, reaches 365. This page counts the Hebrew Bible only.

No. The Hebrew text doesn't come close, however the phrase is counted.
