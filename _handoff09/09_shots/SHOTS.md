# Video 09: available painted images (dark oil-painting style, figures on near-black)

All images are 16:9 paintings. In the edit, each figure is cut out onto pure black and sits on one side of the frame, with graphics on the black side.

## First minute
- 01_jacob_boys: old blind Jacob on the bed, side view, lifting his hands toward the two boys (Manasseh older/taller, Ephraim younger)
- 02_crossing_final: Jacob from the front, arms crossed, right hand on the younger boy (right), left hand on the older boy (left); boys kneeling
- b_joseph: adult Joseph (~56), urgent, reaching out with one hand ("Not so, father")
- 03_jacob_closeup: Jacob's face close-up, eyes almost closed, faint knowing smile ("I know, my son")
- c_twins: young Jacob (dark hair) and Esau (red hair) standing side by side
- 04_isaac: old Isaac alone, seated on a mat
- 05_cain_abel: Cain and Abel standing apart (first two brothers)
- d_300: Gideon's men from behind on a hill at night, torches and shofars, the camp below
- e_offering_fixed: Abel kneeling by his lamb offering in a shaft of light; Cain in shadow with grain
- f_cain_rises: Cain standing over Abel, who sits with his back turned
