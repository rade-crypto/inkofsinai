What does “woman of valor” mean in Hebrew?

Eshet chayil, from Proverbs 31 and the book of Ruth

By Ink of SinaiChecked against the Hebrew textHow we checkPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

“Woman of valor” translates the Hebrew אֵשֶׁת חַיִל (eshet chayil). Chayil means strength, ability and wealth. The Bible also uses it for armies, and for Boaz, “a mighty man of valor.” The phrase appears 3 times in the Hebrew Bible, and only one named woman is called it: Ruth (Ruth 3:11).

The two Hebrew words

אֵשֶׁתwithout vowels: אשתeshet“woman of” or “wife of”
חַיִלwithout vowels: חילchayilstrength · valor · ability · wealth · army

Chayil is a strong word. It's the word for Pharaoh's army, וּבְכָל־חֵילוֹ (Exodus 14:4), for the “capable men” Jethro tells Moses to appoint, אַנְשֵׁי־חַיִל (Exodus 18:21), and for Boaz, גִּבּוֹר חַיִל, “a mighty man of valor” (Ruth 2:1). Near the end of the Proverbs poem it comes back: “many daughters have done valiantly,” רַבּוֹת בָּנוֹת עָשׂוּ חָיִל (Proverbs 31:29).

The poem is an alphabet

Proverbs 31:10–31 has 22 verses, and they begin with the 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order, from א to ת. The woman of valor is praised from the first letter to the last.

The detail most readers miss

The poem that closes Proverbs opens with a question: “a woman of valor, who can find?” In the Leningrad Codex, the oldest complete manuscript of the Hebrew Bible, the very next book is Ruth. There, Boaz says it to her: כִּי אֵשֶׁת חַיִל אָתְּ, “for you are a woman of valor” (Ruth 3:11).

And Boaz was introduced with the same word a chapter earlier: אִישׁ גִּבּוֹר חַיִל, “a man, a mighty man of valor” (Ruth 2:1). The story pairs them with one word before they meet.

Questions people ask

Who is called a woman of valor in the Bible?

Ruth. Boaz says it to her in Ruth 3:11. The other two uses, Proverbs 12:4 and Proverbs 31:10, don't name anyone.

Is “eshet chayil” the same as the Proverbs 31 woman?

Yes. Proverbs 31:10–31 is the Eshet Chayil poem. Each of its 22 verses begins with the next letter of the Hebrew alphabet.

Why is Eshet Chayil sung on Friday night?

Many Jewish families sing Proverbs 31:10–31 at the Shabbat table on Friday night, before Kiddush.

How do you pronounce eshet chayil?

EH-shet KHAH-yil. The “ch” is said like the “ch” in Bach.
