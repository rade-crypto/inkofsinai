[C04] The Hidden Reason Jonah Ran From God
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: Chapter 1 never says why Jonah runs. He only confesses it in chapter 4: he ran because he knew God would forgive Nineveh. Meanwhile the Hebrew tracks him going down: down to Joppa, down into the ship, down into its hold, down to the roots of the mountains.

[C02] Cain and Abel: Why Abel Never Speaks
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: The Bible explains the names of Cain and Seth, but never Abel's. In Hebrew his name is the word for breath or vapor, the same word as Ecclesiastes' 'vanity of vanities'. Abel never says a word in the story. The first time he has a voice, it is his blood crying from the ground.

[C07] The Tower of Babel Was Built for a Name
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: The builders say one thing: 'let us make a name for ourselves.' In Hebrew 'name' is Shem. The very next section is 'these are the generations of Shem', a man literally called 'Name', and that line runs straight to Abram, who is told 'I will make your name great.' The name they tried to build is given to a man told to leave everything.

[C10] The Witch of Endor: The Clue That Told Saul It Was Samuel
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: Saul recognizes the ghost by one thing: 'an old man coming up, wrapped in a robe.' That robe runs through the whole book: Samuel's mother sewed him a little one every year; it tore when Samuel told Saul the kingdom was torn from him; David later cut the corner of Saul's robe. The ghost in the robe repeats the tearing line, and this time names who gets the kingdom: David.

[C13] Ehud and Eglon: The Bible's Locked-Room Murder

[C08] Noah's Ark and Baby Moses' Basket Are the Same Hebrew Word
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: Moses' basket is called by Noah's word for the ark, and the Bible uses that word for nothing else. [C14] The 10 Plagues of Egypt Have a Hidden Pattern
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: The plagues come in three sets of three. And each set answers Pharaoh's first words, 'I do not know Him', with a bigger lesson about what Pharaoh 'will know'.

[C03] The Hidden Reason Samson Lost His Eyes
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: Samson's story runs on his eyes: 'he saw a woman', 'she is right in my eyes'. The Philistines gouge out exactly his eyes, and his last prayer is revenge 'for my two eyes'. Right after him, the book of Judges starts its closing refrain: 'everyone did what was right in his own eyes'. Samson's own phrase becomes the verdict on the whole nation.

[C05] What Most People Miss About Jonah: Nineveh's Evil Lands on Him
     WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS YOU: One Hebrew word runs through Jonah ten times, and English translates it at least four ways. It starts as Nineveh's 'evil', becomes the storm, leaves Nineveh when the city repents, and then lands on Jonah: 'it was evil to Jonah, a great evil'. The book opens with 'their evil' and ends with 'his evil'.

[C12] Bridegroom of Blood: The Hidden Passover in Exodus 4

Zipporah stops it with blood, touching it to 'his feet'. Exodus uses that same 'touch the blood' verb only once more: on Passover night, blood on the doorposts.
