S24 BABEL: FAL STILLS (Nano Banana Pro)

NOT USED in the current cut: the Short is engine-only. These prompts are kept in case stills are wanted later. Nothing gets generated without the owner's OK and a credit estimate first.

HOW TO RUN
Model: fal-ai/nano-banana-pro for the two sheets; fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit for S1–S5, with both sheets passed in image_urls.
Every call: aspect_ratio "5:4", resolution "2K", num_images 1, output_format "png". The sheets use "16:9".
Make the sheets first and approve them. Then make S1–S5 against them.
The engine does everything else: the warm grade, the paper grain, the feathered edges, the slow push-in, all text, and the glowing circles.
So every image has: no text, no letters, no symbols, no logos, no watermark, no frame or border.
God is never shown: no figure, face or hand in the sky.

STYLE (end of every prompt)
Photoreal cinematic film still, ancient Mesopotamia (the plain of Shinar), Early Bronze Age.
One warm low sun as the key light, with long soft shadows.
Amber dust haze in the air. Palette: ochre, umber, sun-baked clay, burnt-brick red, gold highlights. The sky is pale warm haze, never blue.
35mm film grain, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field.
Quiet, serious, epic scale. Modest clothing. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border.

SHEETS
C1. THE BUILDERS (character sheet), on a plain warm-grey background: two men side by side, each in front view, profile and full body. Same lighting in every view.
- THE KILN MASTER: about 50, broad and weathered, sun-dark skin, a short grizzled black beard, a shaved head, deep lines at the eyes. A knee-length undyed wool kilt with a fringed edge, bare chest, a leather cord at the neck. Forearms caked in dried clay.
- THE BRICK CARRIER: about 20, lean, with black curly hair tied back, a thin beard and an earnest open face. A knee-length undyed wool kilt and a rope belt. A leather shoulder pad for carrying bricks.

C2. THE TOWER AND THE CITY (location sheet): the same unfinished tower from three angles: wide from the plain, low angle from its base, and from high above.
- A massive stepped tower of fired mud brick, square in plan, rising in receding terraces. Long ramps of brick run up its sides. Timber scaffolds and rope hoists on the top terrace.
- Its upper terraces are unfinished and lost in haze.
- Around it, a low city of flat-roofed mud-brick houses, brick kilns smoking, stacks of sun-dried bricks, and pots of black bitumen.
- Flat river plain to the horizon, a few date palms.

SHOTS (script order; the plate sits above the text, so keep the subject in the centre and the top third)
S1. "At Babel, the builders say… Come, let us build." (two steps, one slow push-in)
Medium-wide at the brick kilns at golden hour. THE KILN MASTER (C1) presses wet clay into a wooden brick mould on the ground. THE BRICK CARRIER (C1) lifts a stack of fired bricks onto his shoulder pad. Long rows of drying bricks, a kiln glowing orange, a pot of black bitumen. Behind them in the haze, the base of THE TOWER (C2) rises, its first terraces already built, ramps busy with tiny workers.

S2. "A city, and a tower with its top in the sky."
Extreme low angle from the foot of THE TOWER (C2), looking straight up its brick face and ramps. The terraces recede upward and the unfinished top disappears into bright warm haze and cloud. Tiny workers on the ramps for scale. The city's flat roofs crowd the bottom edge of the frame. Awe, vertigo.

S3. "Then God answers with their own word. הָבָה."
Wide, slightly low angle on the unfinished top of THE TOWER (C2) against a vast, heavy evening sky. The clouds above it part, and one broad shaft of warm light falls straight down onto the summit and its scaffolds. Tiny builders on the top terrace stop and look up. Nothing and no one is in the sky: only cloud and light. Stillness before something happens.

S4. "Come, let us go down… and confuse. וְנָבְלָה."
Medium close-up on the top terrace among the scaffolds. THE BRICK CARRIER (C1) holds out a brick and speaks, and THE KILN MASTER (C1) faces him, brow knotted, not understanding a word. Behind them, out of focus, other workers turn to one another with the same lost look, hands raised mid-gesture. A rope hoist hangs still. The warm shaft of light from S3 grazes the scene from above.

S5. "They wanted to make a name. The city got one: בָּבֶל." (two steps, one slow push-in)
Wide at dusk. THE TOWER (C2), unfinished and abandoned: empty ramps, scaffolds left standing, a rope hoist swaying, scattered bricks, cold kilns with no smoke. The last workers walk away across the plain in small separate groups, in different directions. Long shadows, a dimming amber sky, dust settling. Quiet and final.
