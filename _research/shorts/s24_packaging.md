The builders of Babel say, “Let us build.”
God changes one letter, and the building stops.
In Hebrew, they say: הָבָה נִבְנֶה.
Come, let us build.
A city, and a tower with its top in the sky.
Then God answers with their own word. הָבָה.
Come, let us go down… and confuse. וְנָבְלָה.
Put them side by side.
נִבְנֶה. Build.
נָבְלָה. Confuse.
One letter changed. נ became ל.
They wanted to make a name.
The city got one: בָּבֶל.
Because there, God confused the language of all the earth.

INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK CAPTION (also the YouTube Short description)
הָבָה, “come,” opens three sentences in Genesis 11.

The builders say it twice: “Come, let us make bricks” (11:3) and “Come, let us build” (11:4).

Then God says it: “Come, let us go down and confuse” (11:7).

Written in Hebrew, “let us build” is נבנה and “let us confuse” is נבלה.

“Therefore its name was called Babel.” (Genesis 11:9)

For whoever reads the Torah with you.
