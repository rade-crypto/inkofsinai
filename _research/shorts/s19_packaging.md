INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK CAPTION (also the YouTube Short description)
Exodus 20’s Ten Commandments never use the letter ט.

Exodus 20:2–17 has 620 letters, and none of them is a ט.

So the word טוֹב, “good,” can't appear in it.

Deuteronomy 5 repeats the commandments, and the ט comes in twice (5:15 and 5:16).
