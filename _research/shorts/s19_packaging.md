One Hebrew letter is missing from the Ten Commandments.

Exodus 20:2–17 has 620 letters, and not one of them is a ט.

Forty years later, Moses repeats them in Deuteronomy, and the ט shows up twice (5:15 and 5:16).

The second version also adds a line: “so that it may go well with you.”
