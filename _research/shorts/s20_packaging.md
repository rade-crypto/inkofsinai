“Hallelujah” appears 24 times, all in Psalms.

Psalms 146–150 each open and close with it.

Psalm 150 says “praise” 13 times in six verses.

The last verse says who praises: everything that has breath, נְשָׁמָה.

It is the same word as the breath of life in Genesis 2:7.
