GOLIATH VIDEO: IMAGE PROMPTS (Ink of Sinai, video 05)

INSTRUCTIONS FOR GEMINI
Make the images in this file in order, one image per shot, each in 16:9.
Make the 3 character sheets first. Then make every later shot to match them exactly: same faces, hair, armor and clothing.
Apply the STYLE block to every image.
Make 5 images at a time, then stop and wait for me to say "continue".
In every image: no text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermarks. No blood or gore. Modest clothing.

STYLE (apply to every image)
Photoreal cinematic film still, Iron Age Levant, around 1000 BC.
Caravaggio-style chiaroscuro: one hard, warm key light, with shadows falling to near-black.
Palette: deep black, burnished bronze, gold highlights, dusty ochre. Bronze is the warmest thing in the frame.
35mm film grain, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field.
Dust in the light. Epic scale, quiet and serious mood.

CHARACTERS (keep identical in every shot)
GOLIATH: a towering Philistine giant, about twice the height of a normal man.
- Thick black beard, weathered olive skin, cold contemptuous eyes.
- Open-faced bronze helmet. Its rim sits high, so his forehead stays bare.
- A long coat of overlapping bronze scales, laced with leather cords, reaching mid-thigh. Green-black age in the seams.
- Bronze greaves (shin guards) on both shins, their top edges curved like a brow.
- A bronze javelin slung across his back between the shoulders.
- A massive spear with a thick wooden shaft and a dark iron spearhead.
- A huge sword in a sheath at his hip.

DAVID: a slight teenage shepherd, about 16, with a ruddy complexion, auburn curly hair and bright, calm eyes.
- An undyed wool tunic with a rope belt.
- A leather shepherd's bag on a strap.
- A wooden staff and a woven leather sling.
- No armor.

SAUL: the King of Israel. The tallest man in the Israelite camp, middle-aged, with a dark beard going grey.
- A rich dark-red robe over bronze scale armor.
- A worried, tired face.
