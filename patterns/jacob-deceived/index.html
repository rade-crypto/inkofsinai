Why did Jacob deceive his father?

Goats, a garment and the word “recognize”: Genesis 27, 37 and 38 in Hebrew

By Ink of SinaiChecked against the Hebrew textHow we checkPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

Jacob deceived his blind father Isaac because his mother Rebekah told him to. She heard Isaac plan to bless Esau and sent Jacob in his place, dressed in Esau's clothes with goat skins on his hands and neck (Genesis 27:5–17). Years later Jacob's own sons deceived him the same way, with a goat's blood and Joseph's coat (Genesis 37:31–33). The Hebrew repeats the same three things each time: a goat, a garment, and the word “recognize.”

In Genesis 27 the father fails to recognize his son. In Genesis 37 the sons ask their father to recognize the coat, and he does, and believes a lie. In Genesis 38 the same words, הַכֶּר־נָא, “please recognize,” are said to Judah, one of the brothers who had said them to their father, and this time they bring out the truth.

“Deceit” comes back too

When Esau arrives, Isaac tells him: “your brother came בְּמִרְמָה, with deceit” (Genesis 27:35). Then Laban swaps Leah for Rachel on Jacob's wedding night, and Jacob says: “why have you רִמִּיתָנִי, deceived me?” (Genesis 29:25). Same root, now aimed at Jacob.

What “Jacob” means

Jacob was born holding his twin's heel, בַּעֲקֵב עֵשָׂו (Genesis 25:26), and named יַעֲקֹב. After the stolen blessing, Esau plays on the name: “is he not rightly called Jacob? וַיַּעְקְבֵנִי זֶה פַעֲמַיִם, he has supplanted me these two times” (Genesis 27:36).

The story of the stolen blessing is in the Torah portion Toldot (Genesis 25:19–28:9), read this year on November 14. The coat is in Vayeshev, read December 5.

Questions people ask

Why did Jacob deceive his father?

His mother Rebekah heard Isaac plan to bless Esau, told Jacob to bring two goats, and dressed him as Esau (Genesis 27:5–17).

How was Jacob deceived by his sons?

They killed a goat, dipped Joseph's coat in its blood and asked Jacob to recognize it. He concluded Joseph was dead (Genesis 37:31–33).

What does the name Jacob mean in Hebrew?

It comes from עָקֵב, “heel.” Jacob was born holding Esau's heel (Genesis 25:26).

Who deceived Jacob?

Laban, who gave him Leah instead of Rachel (Genesis 29:25), and later his own sons, with Joseph's coat (Genesis 37:31–33).
