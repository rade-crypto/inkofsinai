Why do people in the Bible meet their wives at wells?

The same scene, told three times in the Torah

By Ink of SinaiChecked against the Hebrew textHow we checkPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

Three times in the Torah, a man (or his servant) travels to a foreign land, stops at a well, and meets a young woman who has come for water. Water is drawn, she runs home to tell her family, he is brought into the house, and a marriage follows: Rebekah (Genesis 24), Rachel (Genesis 29) and Zipporah (Exodus 2). The Torah tells it as one scene, repeated, so each version can be read against the others.

What changes

The steps stay the same, so the differences stand out. At the first well, Rebekah draws the water, for a stranger and all ten of his camels. At the second and third, the man draws it: Jacob rolls the stone off the well by himself, and Moses rescues the women from the shepherds before watering their flock. The servant in Genesis 24 is on an errand for someone else. Jacob and Moses are both on the run.

The scene comes back, changed

Later stories echo it without completing it. Boaz tells Ruth to drink from what the young men draw, יִשְׁאֲבוּן הַנְּעָרִים (Ruth 2:9): here the men draw for the woman. And Saul, looking for his father's donkeys, meets girls going out לִשְׁאֹב מָיִם, “to draw water” (1 Samuel 9:11), but no marriage follows. He is anointed king instead.

Jacob meets Rachel at the well in the Torah portion Vayetzei (Genesis 28:10–32:3), read this year on November 21.

Questions people ask

Why are wells important in the Bible?

A well is where people meet: shepherds bring their flocks and young women come to draw water. Three marriages in the Torah begin at one.

Who met their wife at a well in the Bible?

Isaac (through his father's servant, who meets Rebekah, Genesis 24:15–16), Jacob (Rachel, Genesis 29:9) and Moses (Zipporah, Exodus 2:16).

What is a betrothal type scene?

A scene the Bible tells more than once with the same steps: a journey, a well, a young woman, water drawn, a run home, a welcome, a marriage.

Where did Jacob meet Rachel?

At a well in a field, with three flocks of sheep lying beside it (Genesis 29:2).
