The flood story is written as a mirror

The chiasm in Genesis 7–8, counted from the Hebrew

By Ink of SinaiPublished November 8, 2026
Short answer

The days in the flood story count up to a turning point and then back down in reverse: 7 days, 40 days, 150 days, then “God remembered Noah” (Genesis 8:1), then 150 days, 40 days, 7 days, 7 days. A pattern that runs in, turns, and runs back out like this is called a chiasm.

The days, in order

  • 7 days · לְיָמִים עוֹד שִׁבְעָה “in seven more days” Genesis 7:4 · לְשִׁבְעַת הַיָּמִים 7:10
  • 40 days · rain אַרְבָּעִים יוֹם וְאַרְבָּעִים לָיְלָה 7:12 · the flood 7:17
  • 150 days · the waters prevail חֲמִשִּׁים וּמְאַת יוֹם 7:24
  • God remembered Noah · Genesis 8:1
  • 150 days · the waters go down מִקְצֵה חֲמִשִּׁים וּמְאַת יוֹם 8:3
  • 40 days · Noah opens the window מִקֵּץ אַרְבָּעִים יוֹם 8:6
  • 7 days · he waits and sends the dove שִׁבְעַת יָמִים אֲחֵרִים 8:10
  • 7 days · again, and the dove doesn't come back 8:12

The ark and the mountains mirror too

Going inComing out
The water lifts the ark: וַיִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־הַתֵּבָה 7:17The ark comes to rest: וַתָּנַח הַתֵּבָה 8:4
The mountains are covered 7:19–20The mountain tops are seen 8:5

Why build a story this way?

A mirror puts the weight on the middle. Everything before Genesis 8:1 leads to it, and everything after flows from it. In the flood story, the middle is not the rain or the ark. It's “God remembered Noah.”

Questions people ask

What is a chiasm in the Bible?

A passage that runs in one order and then back in reverse (A, B, C, then C, B, A), with the turning point in the middle.

What is the center of the flood story?

“God remembered Noah” (Genesis 8:1). The counts of days go up to it and come back down after it.

How long did it rain during the flood?

Forty days and forty nights (Genesis 7:12).

How long was the flood?

The waters prevailed for 150 days (Genesis 7:24) and went down over another 150 (Genesis 8:3).
