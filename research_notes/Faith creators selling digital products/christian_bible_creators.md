# Christian Bible-Study and Scripture-Insight Creators Selling Digital Products (comparables for @inkofsinai)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Follower counts could NOT be verified for most accounts. Instagram, TikTok, Etsy shop pages and Patreon pages returned 403 to the fetch tool, and Social Blade was not reached. Treat any missing follower count as a gap, not as zero. Self-reported or publisher-reported numbers are flagged **[self-reported]**.

## Which comparable accounts sell digital products, what exactly, and at what price?

### Takeaway
The closest content matches to Ink of Sinai (Torah patterns and Hebrew-letter/word details) earn mainly through **recurring memberships** priced at about $15–20/month. Examples are Aleph Beta, Chaim Bentorah's Hebrew Word Study and Jeff Benner's Patreon. Female-audience Bible-study brands earn mainly through **low-ticket PDFs and printables ($5–$35)** sold on Shopify or Etsy, plus print books. The biggest of those started as one Etsy study (Daily Grace Co.) or one self-published study (Zach Windahl).

### Cited Findings

**A. Torah, Hebrew and "hidden pattern" creators (closest content match)**

1. **Aleph Beta (Rabbi David Fohrman)**: animated Torah videos built on close-reading of patterns and parallels in the Hebrew text.
   - Premium costs **$18/month, or $15/month billed annually** ("17% savings"). A free trial and a money-back guarantee are offered, and a separate "Patron" tier gives deeper perks. — [Aleph Beta pricing](https://www.alephbeta.org/pricing)
   - Premium includes 1,200+ animated videos, podcasts and courses, **printable transcripts and study guides**, webinars with Rabbi Fohrman, and an app. — [Aleph Beta pricing](https://www.alephbeta.org/pricing)
   - The Patrons Circle offers live participation in Fohrman's research and semester-long Biblical Hebrew courses. — [Aleph Beta Patrons Circle](https://www.alephbeta.org/patrons-circle)
   - Aleph Beta is a 501(c)(3), and subscriptions are tax-deductible. — [Aleph Beta pricing](https://www.alephbeta.org/pricing); [subscription page](https://alephbeta.org/premium-subscription)
   - Note: the brand is Jewish, not Christian. It is included because its content model is very close to Ink of Sinai's.

2. **Chaim Bentorah, Hebrew Word Study** (hebrewwordstudy.com, plus a daily word-study blog at chaimbentorah.com):
   - The "All Access" bundle costs **$0.99 for the first month, then $19.99/month** ("rate never goes up").
   - It includes Biblical Hebrew 101, NT Aramaic 101, a Weekly Torah Discussion, In-Depth Word Studies (123 studies in the 2024 set), Psalm 119, Hebrew months and holidays, and a live interactive Bible study.
   - The homepage shows no free email opt-in. The only sign-up is the paid trial.
   - Sources: [hebrewwordstudy.com](https://www.hebrewwordstudy.com/); [chaimbentorah.com word study example](https://www.chaimbentorah.com/2018/05/hebrew-word-study-woman/)

3. **Jeff A. Benner, Ancient Hebrew Research Center**:
   - He runs a Patreon ([patreon.com/jeffbenner](https://www.patreon.com/jeffbenner/about)) and has written over a dozen books on Hebrew language and culture.
   - He also sells a Udemy course, "How to do a Hebrew Word Study Without Knowing Hebrew". — [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/how-to-do-a-hebrew-word-study-without-knowing-hebrew/)
   - A similar "Complete Guide to Hebrew Word Studies" course is sold at Excavating the Bible. — [Excavating the Bible](https://www.excavatingthebible.com/how-to-do-a-hebrew-word-study-without-knowing-hebrew)

### Inferences
- For Ink of Sinai's niche (Torah patterns and Hebrew details), the proven model is a **$15–20/month content library or membership**, as at Aleph Beta and Chaim Bentorah. A mostly female audience at 10k followers would probably also buy **one-off $9–$27 PDFs**, as She Opens Her Bible sells: Hebrew-letter guides, "hidden patterns in Genesis" study guides, Torah-portion journals.
- She Opens Her Bible is the single best template. It is female-focused, sells a $16.98 Hebrew beginner course, bundles printables at $27, and uses a free Hebrew word tool as a lead magnet.
- Daily Grace Co. and Zach Windahl show the scaling path: one study product, then a brand, then print and wholesale.

### Gaps
- **Follower counts with dates** for every account above. Instagram, TikTok and Social Blade were not retrieved, so verify manually.
- No dedicated **faceless** Bible-pattern or numerics short-form account with a verified paid product was found. A search for "faceless Bible TikTok" returned only generic faceless-TikTok guides. One AI-generated Bible account, @holyvlogsz, had 433.4K followers as of Feb 2026 per an academic study, but no product was found. — [Springer study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40839-026-00302-w)

## What revenue evidence exists, and how reliable is it?

### Takeaway
Hard, reliable figures exist only for the nonprofit BibleProject (IRS 990s) and for Patreon estimates (Graphtreon). Everything else is a publisher or company claim about copies sold or subscribers. Aleph Beta's ~10–12k paid subscribers [self-reported] at $15–18/month imply roughly $1.8–2.6M/year, the clearest proof that Torah-pattern content can be monetized.

### Cited Findings
- **BibleProject**: high reliability (IRS Form 990).
  - Revenue was **$30.6M in 2024**, with $26.2M in expenses. — [ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/464277592); [Philanthropy.org](https://philanthropy.org/990/report/464277592/bibleproject)

### Inferences
- Aleph Beta math (my estimate, not a reported figure): 10,000 paying × $180–216/year ≈ **$1.8–2.2M/year** gross, before scholarships and discounts.
- Benner's Patreon shows that a niche Hebrew educator with modest reach makes hundreds, not thousands, per month from Patreon alone. Patreon by itself is weak, and owned products sell better.
- Etsy sales counters (1,445 and 322) at $5–15 each suggest small shops grossing in the low thousands to low tens of thousands lifetime. This is a rough inference that ignores the price mix.

### Gaps
- No public income reports, Kit (ConvertKit) or Stan case studies, or podcast revenue disclosures were found for any female Bible-study creator.
- Aleph Beta's 990 (legal entity name and EIN unknown) was not found.
- No Graphtreon data was retrieved for Aleph Beta (404), Is.It.Written or Cross Bible.

## What funnel do they use from short video to sale?

### Takeaway
The pattern is: a free short video, then a link-in-bio to an owned store (Shopify or Teachable/Thinkific) or a free lead magnet, then an email list, then low-ticket PDFs or a subscription library. Bigger brands then add print books, wholesale and apps. Well-Watered Women's 2026-era pivot shows mature brands moving reliance from social to email.

### Cited Findings
- **She Opens Her Bible**:
  - The funnel runs from social on IG, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest to a free AI Hebrew Word Study Tool lead magnet. — [Hebrew Word Study Tool](https://www.worthbeyondrubies.com/hebrew-word-study/)
  - It then moves to a Shopify store with an email signup and $9–$27 PDFs, and a Thinkific course portal. — [sheopensherbible.net](https://www.sheopensherbible.net/)
- **Aleph Beta**: free videos lead to a free trial, then a $15–18/month subscription with an app, then a Patron tier. Printable study guides are included as part of the value. — [Aleph Beta pricing](https://www.alephbeta.org/pricing)
- **Chaim Bentorah**:
  - A free daily word-study blog leads to a $0.99 first-month trial of a $19.99/month library. — [hebrewwordstudy.com](https://www.hebrewwordstudy.com/); [chaimbentorah.com](https://www.chaimbentorah.com/2018/05/hebrew-word-study-woman/)
  - The trial is a tripwire-priced subscription rather than a free email opt-in.
- **Kristi McLelland**: a self-hosted Teachable course alongside traditionally published Lifeway studies with video access. — [Teachable](https://newlensbiblicalstudies.teachable.com/p/jesus-women); [Lifeway](https://www.lifeway.com/en/product/jesus-and-women-teen-girls-bible-study-book-with-video-access-P005853478)
- **BibleTok creators** commonly point to a free guide via "link in bio". An example is @destinythewonderful's free "How to Read the Bible" guide. — [TikTok discover: Bibletok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/bibletok)
- **Daily Grace Co.**: an Etsy single study grew into its own store, then an app and wholesale on Faire. — [Westminster Bookstore](https://wm.wts.edu/read/the-daily-grace-company); [Faire](https://www.faire.com/brand/b_71egbomogi)
- **Well-Watered Women**: moving away from social toward "high-value emails and evergreen Bible study tools". — [wellwateredwomen.com/next-season](https://wellwateredwomen.com/next-season)
- **Stan Store** is designed to monetize TikTok, IG and YouTube traffic quickly. It hosts multi-module courses with PDFs and costs about $29–$99/month, but lacks quizzes and cohort features. — [Kourses](https://kourses.com/what-is-stan-store/); [Linodash](https://linodash.com/stan-store-review/); [Living Abstracts](https://www.livingabstracts.com/what-is-stan-store/)

### Inferences
- For a faceless ~10k account, a good starter funnel copies She Opens Her Bible and Chaim Bentorah:
  1. A free "Hebrew letter / Torah pattern" PDF lead magnet via link-in-bio (Stan, Beacons or Kit landing page).
  2. An email list.
  3. $9–$27 study guides.
  4. Later, a $10–20/month membership (Patreon or a course platform) of deeper pattern studies.
- The faceless format fits the Aleph Beta and BibleProject animation-led model. Creator personality is not required for content-library monetization.

### Gaps
- Exact link-in-bio tools (Stan, Beacons, Linktree) used by each IG and TikTok account were not verified, because profile pages were not retrievable.
- Conversion rates and email list sizes were not found for any creator.
