# Quran and Islamic Short-Form Creators Who Sell Digital Products

Research date: 2026-10-26. Method: about 15 web searches and fetches. Instagram and TikTok profile pages could not be read directly: instagram.com returned HTTP 429 on fetch. Follower counts below come only from third-party trackers that could be fetched, and each is dated. Revenue figures are rare in this niche. Every revenue claim found is either self-reported or a third-party estimate, and each one is flagged.

## Q1: What do Frames of Noor / Frames of Quran and Quran Marvels style accounts sell, if anything?

### Takeaway
I could not verify these accounts or any products they sell. Web search returned no store, link-in-bio, press or tracker page for "Frames of Noor", "Frames of Quran" or "Quran Marvels". This suggests they are small or mid-size accounts that live on the platforms. Nothing public shows a monetization funnel. The closest monetized relatives I found sell reel-template packs and Quran-reel tools to other creators, not products to their audience.

### Cited Findings
- A search for "Frames of Noor Quran instagram" returned only unrelated Snapchat profiles and generic pages. No account page, store or link tree was found. — [Search result example](https://www.snapchat.com/@nooor.007)
- A search for "framesofquran" / "frames of quran" word count returned only general pages about Quran word counts. Example: scholarly counts range from about 77,277 to 77,934 words depending on how compound words are counted. — [Riwaq Al Quran](https://riwaqalquran.com/blog/how-many-words-in-the-quran/); [Quran Progress](https://www.quranprogress.com/en/blog/how-many-words-in-the-quran/)
- "Quran Marvels" returned only general "numerical marvels" blog posts. One example is a Medium post on the number 19. No account or business turned up. — [Medium: Numerical Marvels](https://medium.com/@awaiskgn/the-numerical-marvels-of-holy-book-quran-55ef2f7466a7); [kaheel7 "Marvels of the Number Seven" PDF](https://www.kaheel7.com/Book/Marvels_BookSeven.pdf)
- Products adjacent to Quran-reel accounts:
  - A Gumroad seller ("deenbound") sells a pack of 100+ Quran recitation reels for about $10. — [deenbound.gumroad.com](https://deenbound.gumroad.com/l/quranreels)
  - A Superprofile page sells a "Quran reels bundle". — [superprofile.bio](https://superprofile.bio/vp/-900--quran-reels-bundle-😍🤲)
  - An iOS app, "Quran Reels Maker", turns an ayah range into a 9:16 video. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760150215)
  - Sellers on Comeup offer custom Quran and duaa reels. — [Comeup](https://comeup.com/fr/service/510270/creer-vos-tiktok-reels-et-shorts-de-versets-du-coran-ou-quran-et-invocations-ou-duaa)
- Instagram follower counts for comparable Quran-reel pages, from a third-party tracker (undated snapshot):
  - quran.timely: about 78.9K — [socialveins](https://socialveins.com/influencer/instagram/quran.timely)
  - quran_ve_ehlibeyt: about 109.6K — [socialveins](https://socialveins.com/influencer/instagram/quran_ve_ehlibeyt)
  - Sheraz Butt (Urdu Quran explainer): about 980.9K, listed on the Motion creator platform — [motionapp.com](https://motionapp.com/creator/isherazbutt/)
- Credibility risk for word-count content: the "numerical miracle" genre is publicly criticized by Muslim scholars. Bilal Philips says critics checking Rashad Khalifa's 19-code found "numerous discrepancies and some outright fabrications". WikiIslam calls apologists' word counts "between questionable and wrong". A journal article says word and letter patterns differ from conventional miracles. — [IslamHouse](https://islamhouse.com/en/articles/190446/); [WikiIslam diff](https://wikiislam.net/wiki/Special:MobileDiff/132871); [journal PDF](https://bfsia.journals.ekb.eg/article_401997_9b88c474eaba176f3b944190203d5877.pdf)

### Inferences
- Faceless Quran-pattern reel pages appear to be mostly unmonetized, or monetized off-platform in ways search does not index (for example, Instagram subscriptions, brand deals or DM sales). No public digital-product funnel was found for the named inspiration accounts.
- The Torah equivalent of the Quran word-count critique (Bible codes and gematria debates) is an obvious risk for Ink of Sinai. Products built on verifiable counts, with sources shown, avoid this weakness.
- The visible "money" in the Quran-reel niche runs partly creator-to-creator: template packs, reel bundles and reel-maker apps.

### Gaps
- I found no handles, follower counts, link-in-bio contents or products for Frames of Noor, Frames of Quran or Quran Marvels. Someone needs to check the profiles manually in the apps; web fetch was rate-limited (429).
- I found no Social Blade, Graphtreon or Stan/Beacons pages for these accounts.

## Q2: Which Quran-content businesses have strong revenue evidence, and how did social video feed them?

### Takeaway
Public revenue figures are almost nonexistent. The strongest monetization evidence is proxy evidence: funding rounds, download counts and subscription prices. The businesses I found follow a ladder: free short or long video, then an app or subscription, then courses or cohorts. Examples are Arabic Unlocked, Bayyinah, Tarteel, Quranic and Niyyah. None has published revenue. The best social-to-product story is Dina Aziz. Her viral free Ramadan planner led to a Penguin Random House book.

### Cited Findings

**1. Bayyinah Institute / Bayyinah TV (Nouman Ali Khan)**
- YouTube: about 2.8M subscribers per Social Blade (2026). Another tracker says about 2.6M subscribers and about 291M views. — [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/bayyinah); [Touchstorm](https://stats.touchstormdatalabs.com/channel-directory/BayyinahInstitute.amigo)
- Product: Bayyinah TV, a subscription video library of Quran and Arabic study. A third-party listing prices it from about $10.99/month (unverified). — [Subger](https://subger.com/de/service/bayyinah-tv)
- Arab News describes Bayyinah as an institute that does not rely on fundraisers. An older article (undated, years old) gives about 4,000 Bayyinah TV subscribers. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/news/445647)
- Case study: [Salaam Gateway](https://salaamgateway.com/story/case-study-bayyinah-tv-helping-muslims-better-understand-their-religion)
- Revenue: Social Blade estimates YouTube ad revenue at $33 to $533 per day (July 2026). This is a third-party estimate, not actual income. No official revenue was found. — [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/bayyinah)

**2. Arabic Unlocked (UK Quranic Arabic)**
- Instagram: 28,699 followers. Tracker refreshed 2026-10-01; followers fell 1.1% over the past year. YouTube is described as the primary platform. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/arabicunlocked/)
- Products: Quranic Arabic app (over 1M downloads, 4.7 stars, per its FAQ, self-reported); Online Academy courses such as the "My Salah Course"; a time-limited cohort "Intensive". — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/arabicunlocked/)
- Funnel: free YouTube lessons (for example, "16 phrases that appear over 450 times in the Quran"), then the app, then the Academy and Intensive. The free lesson is a count-based hook very close to Ink of Sinai's format. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/arabicunlocked/)
- Revenue: none public. CreatorDB estimates $717 to $2K per sponsored Instagram post (third-party estimate). — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/arabicunlocked/)

**3. Tarteel AI (Quran recitation app)**
- Claims more than 15M users and a top-10 app ranking across the GCC. This is self-reported and undated. — [All American Speakers profile of co-founder Mohamed Moussa](https://www.allamericanspeakers.com/speakers/469604/Mohamed-Moussa)
- Accepted to the MIT accelerator in its Fall 2019 cohort. — [Tarteel blog](https://tarteel.ai/blog/tarteel-joins-mit--a-new-chapter/)
- Caplight lists a seed round in Dec 2020. Low confidence: the page mixes in data about another company. — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/tarteel)
- Premium from about $4.99/month per an aggregator (low confidence). — [Subger](https://subger.com/sl/service/tarteel-ai-quran)
- No revenue figures found.

**4. Quranic (getquranic.com, Quranic Arabic learning app)**
- Founder Adam Jamal reports it was the #1 most-funded mobile app on LaunchGood in 2018, raising over 900% of its goal (self-reported). This shows crowdfunding from an existing community as a launch route. — [getquranic.com](https://www.getquranic.com/tag/app/); [AppFollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/quranic-quran-arabic-learning/1381145375?country=mo)

**5. Niyyah (formerly Guider), a daily Quran-understanding app**
- Raised $2M, reported 2024-03-05. Co-led by Venrex and Cur8 Capital, with Active Partners, Emerge and Collective Continuum. About 20,000 users in 70 countries in its first year; content seen by over 1M people. — [Islam Channel](https://islamchannel.tv/?p=15045)
- The source does not describe its social strategy or pricing.

**6. Dina Aziz (@ Instagram; Ramadan Planner), the best viral-planner case**
- Her free digital Ramadan planner went viral, with "over a million downloads in 24 hours" (as reported). Penguin Random House later published it as a hardback book in 2024: *Ramadan Planner: A Guide to Reflection and Growth During the Holy Month*. Over 50K followers, mainly on Instagram. Press coverage ran Feb to Apr 2024. — [Wikipedia: Dina Aziz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dina_Aziz)
- Revenue: none disclosed.

**7. Islamic print and digital planner and journal brands** (evidence is store presence only)
- The Dua Journal sells a Ramadan journal ("Better Than a Thousand Months") and planners and trackers. — [The Dua Journal](https://theduajournal.com/products/better-than-a-thousand-months-ramadan-journal); [collection](https://theduajournal.com/collections/frontpage/islamic-shop)
- Muslim Planner sells an annual "Muslim Planner 2026". — [muslimplanner.com](https://muslimplanner.com/); [products](https://muslimplanner.com/collections/all)
- Other shops: Sweet Moon planners, The Barakah Boutique Ramadan journals, With A Spin Islamic stationery. — [Sweet Moon](https://sweetmoon.us/collections/planners); [Barakah Boutique](https://thebarakahboutique.com/collections/ramadan-journals-planners); [With A Spin](https://shop.withaspin.com/collections/notebooks)
- The Barakah Woman gives away a free digital Ramadan planner (third edition, 2023), a lead-magnet model. — [The Barakah Woman](https://thebarakahwoman.com/2023/03/09/ramadan-planner-third-edition-the-best-free-digital-ramadan-planner/)

**8. Etsy Quran-journal sellers** (named shops ranking for "quran journal", Mar 2026)
- Shops: HelmPaperrco, TahiyatDesigns, Digicarft, HidayaPress, ProductiveMuslimah, DigitallyDeen, rawiajournaling, MindfulMuslimPress, Selfset, ProjectAlIhsan. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/quran-journal)

- Example listings: [Etsy 1391263779](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1391263779), [Etsy 1607538882](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1607538882), [Etsy 1667513504](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1667513504)
- Gumroad equivalents: a Muslim planner PDF from $3, and editable Ramadan planner templates marketed for resale on KDP and Etsy. — [muslimcreatorlab.gumroad.com](https://muslimcreatorlab.gumroad.com/l/ongekw); [truesource.gumroad.com](https://truesource.gumroad.com/l/xpyav)

### Inferences
- The businesses that monetize at scale (Bayyinah, Arabic Unlocked, Tarteel, Quranic) sell education: courses, subscriptions and language apps, not decorative PDFs. Their free content teaches the original-language text. For Ink of Sinai, this points to Hebrew letter and word-study products (a "learn the 50 most frequent Torah words" style course or app, or a Hebrew alphabet workbook). The Arabic Unlocked "16 phrases appear 450 times" lesson is directly analogous to count-based reels.
- Low-ticket printables ($5 to $15) are crowded and commoditized, and resold templates exist. Differentiation would have to come from Ink of Sinai's own data visuals: dot-grid prints, count posters and parasha study journals.
- A free planner can be the biggest growth lever even without direct revenue. The Dina Aziz case led to a traditional book deal.

### Gaps
- No verified revenue for any business here: no Graphtreon/Patreon pages, Sensor Tower or Appfigures revenue estimates, or founder revenue posts turned up in the searches run.
- Etsy shop sales counts were not readable. RankHero shows unlabeled numbers that could not be interpreted.
- I could not check Instagram or TikTok follower counts for the planner brands (Dua Journal, Muslim Planner) or for Dina Aziz beyond "50K+".
- The founder of The Dua Journal and their story were not found.

## Q3: Seasonal (Ramadan) products and how they perform, as an analogue for Jewish holidays

### Takeaway
Ramadan is the clear sales peak for Islamic journals and planners. Etsy search demand for "quran journal" rose about 1.8x in the Jan–Feb pre-Ramadan window of 2026. Viral free Ramadan planners can reach million-download scale. Rosh Hashanah, the Elul/High Holidays, Pesach (Omer counting is like Ramadan tracking) and Shavuot are the natural Jewish counterparts.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy "quran journal" monthly searches were 5,400 for most of the year, then 8,100 in Jan 2026 and a 9,900 peak in Feb 2026, back to 5,400 in March. Ramadan 2026 began around mid-February. That timing link is my inference; the source does not attribute the peak to Ramadan. 2,996 competing listings; keyword difficulty 22/100. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/quran-journal)
- Printable Ramadan planners with Quran, fasting and salah trackers sell at about $1.50 to $8.88 on Etsy. Listings stress A4/A5/Letter sizes, GoodNotes compatibility, "use every year" and multi-child kids' versions. — [Etsy 781872649](https://www.etsy.com/listing/781872649); [Etsy 1044701737](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1044701737); [Etsy 4337905666](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4337905666); [Etsy 4388070376](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4388070376)
- Dina Aziz's free Ramadan planner: over 1M downloads in 24 hours (reported), then a 2024 Penguin Random House book. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dina_Aziz)
- Physical and premium Ramadan journals come from established brands (The Dua Journal's "Better Than a Thousand Months"). — [The Dua Journal](https://theduajournal.com/products/better-than-a-thousand-months-ramadan-journal)
- Free annual "editions" of a Ramadan planner are a recurring lead magnet (The Barakah Woman, third edition 2023). — [The Barakah Woman](https://thebarakahwoman.com/2023/03/09/ramadan-planner-third-edition-the-best-free-digital-ramadan-planner/)

### Inferences
- Product pattern to copy: release a dated yearly edition ("Elul 5787 Journal", "Omer Count Tracker 5787") 4–8 weeks before the holiday, since Etsy demand rose in January ahead of a mid-February Ramadan. Keep a free version for list-building and a paid, better-designed version.
- The 49-day Omer count is the closest structural match to a 30-day Ramadan tracker. A daily counting tracker also fits a dot-grid, counter-style brand.

### Gaps
- No unit sales or revenue for any Ramadan product. Etsy review and sales counts and Gumroad sales numbers were not obtained.
- No direct comparison data on Jewish-holiday product demand (outside this scope).
